## Slide 6: Trend 4 — Sustainability Becomes Market Access

**Visual:** Two stacked cards. Top card: EU regulatory logos or compliance iconography. Bottom card: Supply chain diagram showing travel trade verification flow. "Why It Matters Now" callout box.

**Content blocks:**
- **EU CSRD:** Requires in-scope companies to report on supply chain environmental impact
- **EU CSDDD:** Mandates identification of adverse environmental/human rights impacts in supplier networks
- **The Canadian angle:** Travel trade partners in Europe and Asia must verify supplier credentials — Canadian operators without documentation risk exclusion from international distribution

**Conner:**
> "Here's where this month's scan gets practical for your operator members. The EU's sustainability reporting directives aren't just about European hotels. They require travel trade partners — tour operators, OTAs, travel agencies — to verify their suppliers' credentials. A Canadian hotel or experience provider without documentation may find itself excluded from international distribution channels. This is no longer about guest preferences. It's about market access. DMOs should be helping operators understand what European and Asian partners now require, and connecting them with certification programs."

**Pause point:** "This is no longer about guest preferences — it's about market access."

**Source:** Hospitality Net, June 2026

**Runtime:** ~60 seconds

## Slide 7: Trend 5 — Responsible Tourism Innovation

**Visual:** Banff/Lake Louise mountain imagery. Three program cards: "Park Mode," "Trip Builder," "Cool 2 Carpool." Results callout showing 37,357 items diverted.

**Content blocks:**
- **Park Mode:** Encourages responsible visitor behavior through activation events and rewards
- **Trip Builder:** Digital planning tool that manages expectations and distributes traffic
- **Cool 2 Carpool:** Deeply discounted lift tickets for visitors who share rides
- **Results:** 37,357 single-use items diverted at 2025 Christmas Market; Indigenous tourism career pathways with Stoney Nakoda First Nations

**Conner:**
> "If your destination faces capacity pressure or seasonality challenges, study the Banff model. Banff Lake Louise Tourism has built a suite of visitor management programs that actually shift behavior at scale. Park Mode rewards responsible choices. Trip Builder manages expectations before visitors arrive. Cool 2 Carpool at Mt. Norquay offers steep discounts for ride-sharing. The results are measurable — over 37,000 single-use items diverted at a single event. This is the playbook for destinations where 'more visitors' isn't the goal."

**Pause point:** "This is the playbook for destinations where 'more visitors' isn't the goal."

**Source:** Banff Lake Louise Tourism
