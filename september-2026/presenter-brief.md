## Slide 2: The Big Picture

Four narrative pillars presented in card grid format:

1. **Shoulder season is collapsing** — Fall bookings up 59%, traditional off-peak discounts no longer make sense
2. **Labor crisis is now structural** — 76% of hotels short-staffed despite 15% wage increases since 2020
3. **Business travel fully recovered** — Only 8% shifted to virtual, not the 20-30% predicted
4. **Travel demographics are shifting** — 47% multi-gen trips, 48% seeking crowd-free destinations

**Narrative hook:** "The rules that governed tourism for decades are changing. These aren't temporary disruptions — they're permanent structural shifts."

---

## Slide 3: Trend 1 — Shoulder Season Is Disappearing

**Core stat:** 59% increase in fall bookings year-over-year

**Key data points:**
- September and October bookings up 59%, sales up 77%
- Fall hotel rates now within 10% of peak summer pricing
- Remote work driving year-round travel flexibility
- Climate concerns pushing travelers toward milder shoulder months

**Insight:** "If you're still promoting 'quiet season deals,' you may be underpricing your destination."

**Sources:** Expedia Media, Airbnb Trends 2026

---

## Slide 4: Trend 2 — Labor Crisis Becomes Structural

**Core stat:** 76% of hotels reporting staff shortages

**Key data points:**
- 76% short-staffed despite 15% wage increases since 2020
- 75% of LGBTQ+ hospitality workers faced discrimination
- 53% of Black workers did not feel welcome
- Housekeeping hardest to fill at 82% vacancy rate

**Insight:** "Money alone won't solve this. The talent pipeline is broken because the workplace culture is broken."

**Sources:** AHLA 2026, CGLCC, StatCan Labour Force

---

## Slide 5: Trend 3 — Business Travel Achieves Full Recovery

**Core stat:** $1.62 trillion in global business travel spending

**Key data points:**
- Only 8% of business travel shifted permanently to virtual
- Pandemic predictions said 20-30% would stay virtual
- Face-to-face meetings proved essential for relationship building
- MICE (meetings, incentives, conferences, exhibitions) demand surging

**Insight:** "If you deprioritized corporate travel during the pandemic, reverse course now."

**Sources:** GBTA, Morgan Stanley, CWT

---

## Slide 6: Trend 4 — Multi-Generational Travel Boom

**Core stat:** 47% of travelers now taking multi-generational trips

**Key data points:**
- Up 17 percentage points from 2024
- 85% of families plan multi-gen trips
- 75% of grandparents say travel is the best way to bond with grandchildren
- Gen X grandparents often funding travel for adult children and grandchildren

**Insight:** "If all your imagery shows couples or solo travelers, you're missing the fastest-growing segment."

**Sources:** Campspot 2026 Report, AARP, Travel Weekly

---

## Slide 7: Trend 5 — Destination Dupes Movement

**Core stat:** 48% of travelers want to avoid crowds

**Key data points:**
- "Destination dupes" trending: Albania for Greece, Slovenia for Switzerland, BC wine country for Napa
- Albania hotel prices jumped 14% in one summer
- The dupe lifecycle is accelerating — window between "discovery" and "too expensive" is shrinking
- Opportunity for lesser-known Canadian destinations to position against crowded alternatives

**Insight:** "The dupe positioning only works while you're still affordable. Move fast."

**Sources:** GetYourGuide, Dragonpass

---

## Slide 8: Canada Focus — Air Canada Route Expansion

**Core stat:** 5 new destinations, 7 new routes from western Canada

**Key data points:**
- 5 new Caribbean and Mexican winter sun destinations
- 7 new routes from Vancouver, Calgary, and Edmonton
- Major capacity expansion for western Canadian winter travel
- Signals Air Canada's confidence in outbound winter demand

**Insight:** "If you compete for winter travelers, understand which markets just got easier access to sun destinations."

**Sources:** Air Canada Media

---

## Slide 9: Recommended Actions

Six numbered prescriptive steps:

1. **Rethink shoulder season pricing** — Update forecasting models for 12-month demand curves
2. **Partner on workforce solutions** — Address culture, not just wages; survey operators on barriers
3. **Target business travelers** — Rebuild MICE sales; update corporate travel content
4. **Build multi-gen packages** — Feature three-generation imagery; develop multi-room itineraries
5. **Position as a destination dupe** — Identify your crowded competitor; craft "vibe without crowds" messaging
6. **Leverage Air Canada routes** — Connect with tourism partnerships team; build awareness for new origin markets

---

## Slide 10: If You Do Nothing Else

Three centered action items:

1. **Audit your shoulder-season pricing** — September demand may justify near-peak rates
2. **Survey your operators on workforce barriers** — The crisis affects visitor experience directly
3. **Update your imagery to show multi-gen travelers** — 47% of the market, growing fast

## Narrative Throughline

The presentation asks: "Are you prepared for permanent changes in how tourism works?"

It emphasizes that:
- Seasonality is flattening (pricing opportunity)
- Labor problems are cultural, not just economic (retention over recruitment)
- Business travel is back (rebuild MICE capacity)
- Family structures are driving new travel patterns (multi-gen is mainstream)
- Crowd avoidance creates positioning opportunities (but windows are short)

**Tone:** Pragmatic, data-driven, action-oriented. Less about marketing tactics, more about structural business decisions.
