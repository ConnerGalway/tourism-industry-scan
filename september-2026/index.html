Shoulder Season Is Disappearing

Economics

The traditional fall "shoulder season" has officially shed its bargain reputation. Fall 2026 bookings are up 59% with sales up 69%, changing how destinations price their seasonal offerings.

Rate Surge Data

French Riviera hotel rates jumped +179% for fall bookings. Puglia, Italy up +78%. Across top 10 US markets, fall lodging costs are now 20% above summer levels.

Remote work has enabled this shift. Travelers now choose "cheaper days" flexibly, spreading demand into what were once slower periods. Vrbo reports a 17% YoY increase in fall travel interest, with average nightly rates now only 5% below peak summer.

September bookings +55%, sales +77%
October bookings +59%
November bookings +70%
Europe fall bookings +49% despite +7% rates
Avg domestic ticket $326 (+39% YoY)

Sources: Expedia Fall Outlook, CNBC, Vrbo

Labor Crisis Becomes Structural

Workforce

The hospitality labor shortage is no longer cyclical. It's now a long-term reality. 76% of US hotels are operating short-staffed, with the industry facing an 8.6 million worker shortfall by 2035.

Scale of the Crisis

65% of US hotels report ongoing shortages. 71% have positions they cannot fill despite active recruiting. Hotels average 6-7 open roles per property.

AHLA projects US hotels will pay $131 billion in wages and benefits in 2026. Most acute gaps: housekeeping, front desk, culinary, and maintenance. Globally, tourism worker demand will exceed supply by 43 million by 2035.

Hotels operating short-staffed 76%
Hotels with unfillable positions 71%
2026 industry wages $131 billion
Hospitality gap by 2035 8.6 million (18% below need)
Global tourism gap by 2035 43 million

Sources: AHLA State of Industry 2026, WTTC Workforce Report

Business Travel Achieves Full Recovery

Economics

Global business travel spending is forecast to reach $1.62 trillion in 2026, marking the sector's full recovery from pandemic disruption. Virtual meeting substitution has collapsed to just 8%, down from projections as high as 29%.

Recovery Indicators

MICE projected to grow 7-9% in 2026. 61% of travel managers optimistic about outlook (up from 50%). SMEs now drive 50%+ of all new managed travel programs.

The pandemic-era prediction that virtual meetings would permanently replace 20-30% of business travel has proven wrong. Face-to-face interaction remains essential for relationship building, deal-making, and complex collaboration.

Global business travel spend $1.62 trillion
Virtual substitution rate 8% (vs 29% projected)
MICE growth forecast 7-9%
Hotel bookings (business) +6.3%
Room rates (business) +3.9%

Sources: GBTA, Morgan Stanley, CWT

Destination Dupes Movement

Consumer

"Destination dupes" has emerged as a major travel trend in 2026: affordable alternatives to famous, expensive, or overcrowded hotspots. 48% of travelers say they want to avoid crowds in 2026. But dupes don't stay affordable long.

The Dupe Lifecycle

Discovery → viral content → price surge → new dupe needed. Albania hotel prices jumped 14% in one summer vs 2% in Greece. The window is narrowing fast.

The trend surged with Venice's entry fee (April 3) and Japan's new tourist levies (July 1). Popular swaps: Albania for Greece, Slovenia for Switzerland, Oman for Maldives. 38% cite money-saving as primary motivation.

Travelers avoiding crowds 48%
Money-saving motivation 38%
Albania price increase +14% (vs Greece +2%)
Venice entry fee start April 3, 2026

Sources: GetYourGuide, Dragonpass

Multi-Generational Travel Boom

Consumer

Multi-generational travel has surged: 47% of all travelers in 2026 are opting for multi-gen trips, a 17 percentage point increase from 2024. Gen X grandparents are often funding these journeys.

XZ Beta Travel

New pattern emerging: Gen X grandparents vacationing with Gen Z adult children (young parents) and Beta babies (born 2026+). 58% of Millennial/Gen Z parents plan to bring extended family.

AARP reports 75% of grandparents say family travel is the single best way to bond with grandchildren. Multi-gen groups have complex needs: accessibility, kid-friendly options, shared and separate spaces.

Multi-gen travelers 47% (+17 pts from 2024)
Families planning multi-gen 85%
Parents with grandparents 57%
Extended family vacations +48% (+7 pts from 2023)

Sources: Campspot 2026, AARP, Travel Weekly

Air Canada Route Expansion

Canada Focus

On September 3, 2026, Air Canada announced a major global expansion: 5 new international destinations and 7 new routes for Summer 2027, representing 8%+ increase in available seat miles.

Key New Routes

Toronto: Oslo, Shannon, Nice. Vancouver: Guangzhou (year-round). Plus Toronto-London Heathrow day-tripper and daily Toronto-Shanghai service.

The Guangzhou route stands out. Year-round service to China's third-largest city opens a major source market for Canadian tourism. Air Transat also expanded with Quebec City-Marseille and Ottawa-London Gatwick.

Sources: Air Canada

04

Recommended Actions

Six strategic moves for DMO leaders responding to structural shifts in Canadian tourism. Each action includes budget-appropriate approaches that scale from regional DMOs to provincial organizations.

01

Rethink shoulder season pricing and capacity planning

The Evidence

Fall bookings jumped 59% year-over-year, with sales up 77%. September now generates rates within 10% of peak summer. The traditional off-season discount no longer makes sense when demand matches July.

What Changes In Your Week

Provincial DMOs: Update visitor forecasting models to reflect 12-month demand curves. Coordinate with operators on dynamic pricing strategies. Regional DMOs: Work with accommodation partners to test higher fall rates. Track conversion impact carefully. Any budget: Audit your current shoulder-season messaging. If you're still promoting "quiet season deals," you may be underpricing your destination.

RevenuePlanning
02

Partner with operators on workforce solutions, not just marketing

The Evidence

76% of hotels report staff shortages despite 15% wage increases since 2020. 75% of LGBTQ+ hospitality workers faced discrimination. 53% of Black workers did not feel welcome. The talent pipeline is broken.

What Changes In Your Week

Provincial DMOs: Add workforce development to your mandate. Partner with hospitality schools and immigration settlement agencies. Regional DMOs: Host operator roundtables focused on retention, not recruitment. Address workplace culture. Any budget: Survey your operators on their biggest hiring barriers. Share findings collectively. The workforce crisis affects visitor experience directly.

WorkforceOperations
03

Target business travelers with renewed urgency

The Evidence

Only 8% of business travel shifted to virtual, far below the 20-30% pandemic predictions. Global business travel spending hit $1.62 trillion. Face-to-face meetings proved irreplaceable for relationship building and deal-making.

What Changes In Your Week

$100K+ budget: Rebuild your meetings and conventions sales team. Invest in MICE (meetings, incentives, conferences, exhibitions) infrastructure positioning. $25K-100K: Partner with local convention centres and hotels on joint business travel packages. Under $25K: Update your business traveler content. If you deprioritized corporate travel during the pandemic, reverse course now.

Business TravelRevenue
04

Build multi-generational travel packages and experiences

The Evidence

47% of travelers now take multi-generational trips, up 17 percentage points from 2024. 85% of families plan multi-gen trips. 75% of grandparents say travel is the best way to bond with grandchildren.

What Changes In Your Week

$50K+ budget: Develop multi-gen itineraries with operators. Feature accommodations with multi-room configurations. Create content showing three generations together. Under $50K: Audit your imagery. If all your photos show couples or solo travelers, you're missing the fastest-growing segment. Feature grandparents with grandchildren in your next campaign.

FamiliesExperiences
05

Position as a destination dupe before the window closes

The Evidence

48% of travelers want to avoid crowds. "Destination dupes" are trending: Albania for Greece, Slovenia for Switzerland, BC wine country for Napa. But the dupe lifecycle is accelerating. Albania hotel prices jumped 14% in one summer.

What Changes In Your Week

Any budget: Identify what overcrowded destination your region resembles. Craft messaging around "the vibe without the crowds." Warning: If you're already popular, this trend works against you. Focus on dispersal to lesser-known areas within your region instead. The dupe positioning only works while you're still affordable.

PositioningStrategy
06

Leverage Air Canada's winter route expansion

The Evidence

Air Canada added 5 new winter sun destinations (Caribbean and Mexico) and 7 new routes from Vancouver, Calgary, and Edmonton. This represents a major capacity expansion for western Canadian winter travel.

What Changes In Your Week

Western Canadian DMOs: Connect with Air Canada's tourism partnerships team. Explore joint marketing on new routes. Inbound destinations: If your destination gained new Air Canada service, build awareness campaigns targeting the new origin markets. Outbound impact: If you compete for winter travelers, understand which markets just got easier access to sun destinations and adjust your value proposition.
