Shoulder Season Is Disappearing

Economics

The traditional fall "shoulder season" has officially shed its bargain reputation. Fall 2026 bookings are up 59% with sales up 69%, changing how destinations price their seasonal offerings.

Rate Surge Data

French Riviera hotel rates jumped +179% for fall bookings. Puglia, Italy up +78%. Across top 10 US markets, fall lodging costs are now 20% above summer levels.

Remote work has enabled this shift. Travelers now choose "cheaper days" flexibly, spreading demand into what were once slower periods. Vrbo reports a 17% YoY increase in fall travel interest, with average nightly rates now only 5% below peak summer.

Europe fall bookings +49% despite +7% rates
Avg domestic ticket $326 (+39% YoY)

Sources: Expedia Fall Outlook, CNBC, Vrbo

Labor Crisis Becomes Structural

Workforce

The hospitality labor shortage is no longer cyclical. It's now a long-term reality. 76% of US hotels are operating short-staffed, with the industry facing an 8.6 million worker shortfall by 2035.

Scale of the Crisis

65% of US hotels report ongoing shortages. 71% have positions they cannot fill despite active recruiting. Hotels average 6-7 open roles per property.

AHLA projects US hotels will pay $131 billion in wages and benefits in 2026. Most acute gaps: housekeeping, front desk, culinary, and maintenance. Globally, tourism worker demand will exceed supply by 43 million by 2035.

Sources: AHLA State of Industry 2026, WTTC Workforce Report

Business Travel Achieves Full Recovery

Economics

Global business travel spending is forecast to reach $1.62 trillion in 2026, marking the sector's full recovery from pandemic disruption. Virtual meeting substitution has collapsed to just 8%, down from projections as high as 29%.

Recovery Indicators

MICE projected to grow 7-9% in 2026. 61% of travel managers optimistic about outlook (up from 50%). SMEs now drive 50%+ of all new managed travel programs.

The pandemic-era prediction that virtual meetings would permanently replace 20-30% of business travel has proven wrong. Face-to-face interaction remains essential for relationship building, deal-making, and complex collaboration.

Global business travel spend $1.62 trillion
Virtual substitution rate 8% (vs 29% projected)
MICE growth forecast 7-9%
Hotel bookings (business) +6.3%
Room rates (business) +3.9%

Sources: GBTA, Morgan Stanley, CWT

Destination Dupes Movement

Consumer

"Destination dupes" has emerged as a major travel trend in 2026: affordable alternatives to famous, expensive, or overcrowded hotspots. 48% of travelers say they want to avoid crowds in 2026. But dupes don't stay affordable long.

The Dupe Lifecycle

Discovery → viral content → price surge → new dupe needed. Albania hotel prices jumped 14% in one summer vs 2% in Greece. The window is narrowing fast.

The trend surged with Venice's entry fee (April 3) and Japan's new tourist levies (July 1). Popular swaps: Albania for Greece, Slovenia for Switzerland, Oman for Maldives. 38% cite money-saving as primary motivation.

Travelers avoiding crowds 48%
Money-saving motivation 38%
Albania price increase +14% (vs Greece +2%)
Venice entry fee start April 3, 2026

Sources: GetYourGuide, Dragonpass

Multi-Generational Travel Boom

Consumer

Multi-generational travel has surged: 47% of all travelers in 2026 are opting for multi-gen trips, a 17 percentage point increase from 2024. Gen X grandparents are often funding these journeys.

XZ Beta Travel

New pattern emerging: Gen X grandparents vacationing with Gen Z adult children (young parents) and Beta babies (born 2026+). 58% of Millennial/Gen Z parents plan to bring extended family.

AARP reports 75% of grandparents say family travel is the single best way to bond with grandchildren. Multi-gen groups have complex needs: accessibility, kid-friendly options, shared and separate spaces.

Multi-gen travelers 47% (+17 pts from 2024)
Families planning multi-gen 85%
Parents with grandparents 57%
Extended family vacations +48% (+7 pts from 2023)

Sources: Campspot 2026, AARP, Travel Weekly

Air Canada Route Expansion

Canada Focus

On September 3, 2026, Air Canada announced a major global expansion: 5 new international destinations and 7 new routes for Summer 2027, representing 8%+ increase in available seat miles.

Key New Routes

Toronto: Oslo, Shannon, Nice. Vancouver: Guangzhou (year-round). Plus Toronto-London Heathrow day-tripper and daily Toronto-Shanghai service.

The Guangzhou route stands out. Year-round service to China's third-largest city opens a major source market for Canadian tourism. Air Transat also expanded with Quebec City-Marseille and Ottawa-London Gatwick.

04

Recommended Actions

Six marketing moves a DMO executive director can make this quarter, drawn directly from this month's source set. Each action includes budget-appropriate approaches that scale from regional DMOs to provincial organizations.

01

Shift to short-form video as your primary content format

The Evidence

70% of Gen Z/Millennials find destinations through short-form video. A 15-second behind-the-scenes reel outperformed a professional property tour by 340% on reach. Short-form drives 2.8x more engagement than static ads.

What Changes In Your Week

$50K+ budget: Hire a part-time content creator or reallocate 20% of video production budget to short-form. Under $50K: Train existing staff on smartphone content capture. Use free tools like CapCut for editing. Prioritize quantity and authenticity over production value. Your iPhone is enough.

02

Test micro-influencer partnerships with long-term relationship goals

The Evidence

Micro-influencers (10K-250K followers) generate $6 average ROI per $1 spent. 82% of travelers trust creator recommendations over traditional ads. Booking conversion averages 5% vs 0.5% for display. 92% of DMOs increasing or maintaining influencer budget.

What Changes In Your Week

$25K+ budget: Partner with 2-3 micro-influencers for a multi-visit relationship (3+ trips over 12 months). Under $25K: Identify local creators already posting about your region and offer value exchange (experiences, not cash). Start with nano-influencers (1K-10K) who have genuine regional connection. Remember: disclosure is required under Ad Standards Canada.

03

Audit your social response rate and set a 24-hour reply target

The Evidence

Travel brands respond to only 6% of incoming social messages. 94% of interactions, many from high-intent travelers, go unanswered. Meanwhile, 64% are comfortable booking directly through social platforms.

What Changes In Your Week

Any budget: This action costs nothing but staff time. Audit response rates this week using native platform analytics. $25K+ budget: Consider a social listening tool (Sprout Social, Hootsuite) to centralize and track response times. Under $25K: Assign one team member "social duty" each day. Set phone alerts for DMs. Commit to 24-hour reply windows. This is the simplest improvement in destination marketing.

04

Run full-funnel campaigns that connect awareness to booking

The Evidence

Awareness focus dropped from 59% to 25% in one year. 79% of North American DMOs now prioritize ROI. Conversions as main goal jumped from 15% to 31%. 47% of DMOs running full-funnel campaigns.

What Changes In Your Week

$100K+ budget: Restructure into unified campaigns with awareness, consideration, and conversion phases tracked together. Invest in attribution tools to connect media spend to booking outcomes. $25K-100K: Start with one integrated campaign per season. Use UTM parameters and Google Analytics to track the full journey. Under $25K: Focus on fewer, better-tracked initiatives. Even manual tracking (ask "how did you hear about us?") beats flying blind on attribution.

05

Test TikTok Travel Ads for full-funnel conversion

The Evidence

TikTok Travel Ads launched September 2025 with Hotels, Flights, and Destinations catalogue types. Powered by Smart+ AI engine. It's now a full-funnel tool, not just awareness. 60% of users visit new destinations after TikTok discovery.

What Changes In Your Week

$50K+ budget: Run a full TikTok Travel Ads test with destination catalogue campaigns. Minimum recommended spend: $10K/month for 3 months to gather meaningful data. $15K-50K: Start with Spark Ads (boosting organic creator content) rather than building campaigns from scratch. Lower barrier, similar authentic feel. Under $15K: Focus on organic TikTok presence first. Build content library before paid investment. Note: Monitor TikTok's regulatory status in Canada before major commitments.

06

Study the Montreal model: bold, differentiated creative that owns a position

The Evidence

Montreal's "Reality TV" campaign with Cossette drove record 12.5M visitors and 82% hotel occupancy. Penn Station activation reached American commuters. Clear positioning: "Toronto is the downtown of Canada. Montreal is the playground."

What Changes In Your Week

Any budget: The positioning exercise costs nothing. Block 2 hours this month to define your destination's ownable position. Ask: "What can we own that competitors can't?" $50K+ budget: Once you have positioning clarity, invest in one bold creative execution per year rather than many mediocre ones. Under $50K: Look at Tourism Saskatoon's "Seriously Underrated" campaign (est. $400K), proof that positioning clarity, not budget size, drives differentiation.
