## Slide 3: Trend 1. Travel Costs Are Running at 2x Inflation

**Core stat:** Travel Price Index +8.7% YoY vs +3.4% overall CPI

**Key data points (U.S. Travel Association, October 2026):**
- Airfares: +25.5% year-over-year in September 2026
- Gasoline: +27.4% YoY, making road trips more expensive
- Hotel prices: Remain elevated despite slight monthly dip
- Recreation services: Up 7.2% annually
- Travel Price Index rose 2.1% in September alone, the largest monthly gain since March

**Behavioral impact:**
- Air trips expected to decline 0.8% in 2026
- Car/other mode trips rise 1.9% as travelers switch to cheaper options
- 52% say they'll still travel but adjust plans (Skift State of Travel 2026)
- Only 1% planning to postpone or cancel travel entirely

**Insight:** "Higher costs are changing *how* Americans travel, not *whether* they travel. The winners will be destinations accessible by car within drive markets."

**Sources:** [U.S. Travel Association Travel Price Index](https://www.ustravel.org/research/travel-price-index), [Skift State of Travel 2026](https://skift.com/insights/state-of-travel/)

## Slide 4: Trend 2. Affluent Travelers Are Carrying the Industry

**Core stat:** Revenue growth outpacing volume growth by 2:1 (Deloitte Corporate Travel Study 2026)

**Evidence from Q3 2026 earnings:**
- **Booking Holdings:** Room nights +7%, but gross bookings +10%; price outpacing volume
- **Expedia:** Booked room nights +7%, but revenue +12%; B2B growing 3x faster than B2C
- **Airbnb:** ADR +4-6% across regions; mobile app bookings +29% YoY
- **American Express:** Travel & entertainment spending +10%; airline category +13%

**Consulting firm validation:**
- McKinsey: Luxury travel growing at 6% CAGR, outpacing broader travel by 2 percentage points
- BCG: Top-tier luxury clients (€50K+ annual spend) increased share of total luxury spend from 14% to 24%
- Deloitte: "Cautious class" of high-income Americans ($200K+) planning fewer but more conservative trips

**Skift analysis:**
- "Revenue growth is increasingly carried by higher prices and a smaller group of affluent travelers rather than by rising volumes"
- Skift Travel Health Index stood at 100 in August, indicating flat YoY growth in volume

**Insight:** "The market is bifurcating. Premium positioning and high-value traveler acquisition are now essential, not optional."

**Sources:** [Booking Holdings Q3 2026 SEC Filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001075531/000107553126000044/), [Expedia Q3 2026 SEC Filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001324424/000132442426000059/), [Deloitte 2026 Travel Industry Outlook](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/transportation/travel-hospitality-industry-outlook.html), [McKinsey State of Consumer 2026](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/consumer-packaged-goods/our-insights/state-of-consumer)

## Slide 5: Trend 3. AI Planning Hits Inflection Point

**Core stat:** 56% of U.S. leisure travelers used AI in 2026, up from 43% nine months prior

**Adoption data (multiple sources):**
- Phocuswright: 43% to 56% adoption, which they call "the fastest behavioral shift the travel industry has seen in more than a decade"
- Deloitte: GenAI use for trip planning rose from 15% (2025) to 25% (2026)
- High-income millennials: 43% adoption rate (Deloitte)
- 58% of U.S. summer travelers prioritize AI tools over traditional travel websites

**The trust gap (Klook Travel Pulse 2026):**
- 91% use AI as a trip planning tool
- Only 2% trust AI to actually book without human oversight
- Primary uses: Recommendations (75%), itinerary planning (70%), discovery (69%)
- Booking: Only 13% expect to use AI for this

**Generational splits (Simon-Kucher 2026):**
- Under 45: 65% would use AI for recommendations
- Gen X: 44%
- Boomers: 29%

**Insight:** "AI has crossed the chasm for trip planning. But the booking moment still requires human trust. The opportunity is in the handoff."

**Sources:** [PhocusWire Travel Forward 2026](https://www.phocuswire.com/phocuswright-travel-forward-report-2026), [Deloitte 2026 Travel Outlook](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/transportation/travel-hospitality-industry-outlook.html), [Klook Travel Pulse 2026](https://www.klook.com/newsroom/travelpulse-2026-biggerbudgets-boldertrips/)

## Slide 6: Trend 4. OTAs Prove AI Users Convert at 2x the Rate

**Core stat:** Booking Holdings AI assistant users convert at 2x the rate of non-users (Q3 2026 earnings)

**From Booking Holdings Q3 2026 earnings call:**
- AI assistant users at Priceline and Agoda convert at about twice the rate of non-users
- Agoda AI assistant users have average booking values ~20% higher
- Company "continues making progress integrating AI across its brands"

**Platform integrations announced:**
- Google working with Booking.com, Expedia, Marriott, IHG, Choice, and Wyndham to bring hotel bookings directly into AI Mode
- ChatGPT, Copilot, Perplexity, and Gemini can now assemble multi-supplier trips and execute bookings

**DMO response (Sojern State of Destination Marketing 2026):**
- 64% of DMOs now write content specifically formatted for AI engines
- 51% are either very concerned about generative AI's impact or actively developing a response strategy

**Insight:** "The conversion data is in. AI users are better customers. The question isn't whether to optimize for AI. It's how fast you can adapt your content strategy."

**Sources:** [Booking Holdings Q3 2026 Earnings](https://www.phocuswire.com/news/finance/booking-holdings-q3-2026-earnings), [Sojern State of Destination Marketing 2026](https://www.sojern.com/reports/state-of-destination-marketing-2026)

## Slide 7: Trend 5. Global Outlook Diverges by Region

**Core stat:** 1.57 billion international arrivals forecast for 2026 (+2% YoY), but 2027 expected to accelerate to +8%

**UNWTO September Barometer:**
- H1 2026 growth: Just 0.4% (690 million arrivals), the slowest since recovery began
- Middle East conflict and elevated oil prices cited as primary drags
- Full-year 2026 outlook revised down from 3-4% growth to 1-2%

**Regional performance (UNWTO, Euromonitor, WTTC):**
| Region | 2026 Growth | 2027 Forecast |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| Europe | +5.6% | +5% |
| Africa | +4% | +5.4% |
| Americas | +2% | +3% |
| Asia-Pacific | +1% (still 11% below 2019) | +5.4% |
| Middle East | -7.5% (Sept) | +43% rebound |

**WTTC headline:**
- Travel & Tourism GDP growing at 4.1% globally, outpacing world economy at 2.8%
- $12 trillion contribution to global economy forecast for 2026

**Canadian context:**
- Q1 2026: 78.7 million domestic trips (+1.5% YoY)
- Overseas visitor spending in Canada +10.2% to $2.1 billion
- August arrivals +4.3% YoY

**Insight:** "2026 is a holding year. 2027 is when the rebound accelerates, particularly in Asia-Pacific and Middle East once conflicts ease."

**Sources:** [UNWTO World Tourism Barometer September 2026](https://www.untourism.int/un-tourism-world-tourism-barometer-data), [WTTC Economic Impact Research 2026](https://researchhub.wttc.org/), [Statistics Canada Leading Indicator August 2026](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260911/dq260911b-eng.htm)

## Slide 8: Trend 6. The B2B Pivot Is Real

**Core stat:** Expedia B2B bookings +19% vs B2C +7% (Q3 2026)

**From Expedia Q3 2026 earnings:**
- B2B gross bookings grew 19%, B2C grew just 7%
- B2B revenue growth of 23% "showcases durability and high demand for Expedia's backend travel technology platform"
- Strategy: Becoming a "one-stop travel shop" for partners
- Recent acquisition of CarTrawler and launch of new activities APIs

**Industry-wide shift:**
- OTA bookings generated $408 billion in 2026 (PhocusWright)
- Online gross bookings reaching $1.2 trillion by end of 2026
- 65% of global travel bookings now made online

**PhocusWright insight:**
- Tours and activities only 33% online booked vs 64% for travel overall, leaving massive runway for digitization
- Global experiences market: $253 billion in 2024, projected $342 billion by 2029

**Insight:** "The next growth wave for travel platforms isn't consumer acquisition. It's powering other businesses' travel capabilities. If you're a DMO, your tech stack partner matters more than ever."

**Sources:** [Expedia Q3 2026 SEC Filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001324424/000132442426000059/), [PhocusWright Travel Forward 2026](https://www.phocuswright.com/Travel-Research/Research-Updates/2026/Research-Roundup-3Q26)

## Slide 9: Data Points That Matter

Deep-cut statistics most people won't have seen:

**Consumer behavior:**
- 81% of travelers would choose destination based on how they want to feel, not where (Booking.com 2027)
- 66% want packages that let them remove services they won't use (Booking.com 2027)
- Pet-friendly bookings +260% YoY (Tripadvisor Trendcast 2026)
- Marathon tourism: +300% arrivals for Chicago Marathon, +228% for Boston (Tripadvisor)

**Loyalty shifts:**
- 41% of U.S. travelers prefer hotel loyalty programs over travel credit cards (Forbes Advisor 2026)
- Hilton added Diamond Reserve tier; reduced Gold to 25 nights, Diamond from 60 to 50

**Search behavior:**
- Google travel searches down ~10% throughout 2026 even as travel volume holds steady; AI may be capturing discovery
- Flight transaction value: +25% Canada, +20% Italy, +17% Japan, -21% UK (Skift Travel Health Index)

**Cruise industry:**
- 39.1 million cruise passengers forecast for 2026, 42.1 million by 2029 (CLIA)
- 31% of cruisers in past two years were new-to-cruise, up 12 percentage points

**Short-term rental:**
- U.S. STR occupancy averaging 57.4%, above pre-pandemic 57.0%
- RevPAR +2.9% in 2026, driven entirely by rate (occupancy flat)

**Insight:** "The data reveals a market in transition. Search is shifting to AI. Loyalty is shifting to hotels. Experiences are shifting to active and pet-friendly. The question is whether your positioning reflects where travelers are going, not where they were."

**Sources:** [Tripadvisor Trendcast 2026](https://tripadvisor.mediaroom.com/2026-01-28-Tripadvisor-Trendcast-2026-Introduces-The-Year-of-Meaningful-Travel-Experiences), [Forbes Advisor Loyalty Survey 2026](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/r4rjpih9w3re/), [CLIA State of Cruise Industry 2026](https://cruising.org/resources/state-cruise-industry-report-2026), [AirDNA/SkyRun STR Data 2026](https://skyrun.com/blog/short-term-rental-market-outlook/)

## Slide 10: Recommended Actions

Six numbered prescriptive steps based on the data:

1. **Optimize for the affluent traveler:** With revenue growth outpacing volume 2:1, premium positioning and high-value guest acquisition are essential; consider tiered experiences
2. **Make your content AI-legible:** 64% of DMOs are already formatting content for AI; test your destination in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode
3. **Build the AI-to-booking handoff:** 91% plan with AI, 2% book with it; the conversion opportunity is in bridging that gap with seamless human confirmation
4. **Re-evaluate your channel mix:** Display advertising dropped from 75% to 45% of DMO spend; YouTube grew to 55%; TikTok fell from 49% to 28%
5. **Prepare for 2027 acceleration:** International arrivals forecast to grow 8% next year vs 2% this year; Asia-Pacific and Middle East recovery will drive demand
6. **Consider B2B partnerships:** Expedia's B2B is growing 3x faster than B2C; your distribution strategy may need to include platform partnerships

## Slide 11: If You Do Nothing Else

Three centered action items:

1. **Run the AI audit:** Query ChatGPT and Perplexity with "[your destination] vacation planning." Is the response accurate, helpful, and bookable?
2. **Know your affluent traveler:** Pull your top 20% of guests by spend and analyze their behavior; they're now driving industry growth
3. **Watch the 2027 forecasts:** If you're planning campaigns for next year, build in capacity for the rebound, especially from Asia-Pacific and Middle East source markets
