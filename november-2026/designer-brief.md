# November 2026 Tourism Industry Scan: Designer Brief

**Date:** 2026.11.12
**Presenter:** Conner Galway
**Runtime:** ~12 minutes
**Format:** 12 slides, 16:9 aspect ratio
**Design palette:** Navy/cream with accent colors (consistent with previous months)

---

## Theme: The Price-Value Paradox

This month synthesizes Q3 earnings from OTAs, consulting firm research, and industry association data. The visual tone should feel authoritative and data-driven. Heavy use of statistics and charts.

---

## Slide 1: Cover

**Headline:** November 2026 Tourism Industry Scan
**Subhead:** The Price-Value Paradox
**Visual:** Vol. 8 badge, navy/cream palette with oversized headline

Four narrative pillars in card grid format:

## Slide 3: Trend 1. Travel Costs Are Running at 2x Inflation

**Hero stat:** 8.7% (Travel Price Index YoY increase)
**Comparison stat:** 3.4% (overall CPI)
**Tag:** Economics

**Key data points to visualize:**
- Airfares: +25.5% YoY
- Gasoline: +27.4% YoY
- TPI rose 2.1% in September alone (largest monthly gain since March)

**Behavioral insight callout:** "52% will adjust plans. Only 1% will postpone entirely."

**Visual direction:** Bar chart comparing TPI vs CPI, or stacked metrics showing cost components

**Source:** U.S. Travel Association, Skift State of Travel 2026

---

## Slide 4: Trend 2. Affluent Travelers Are Carrying the Industry

**Hero stat:** 2:1 (revenue growth vs volume growth ratio)
**Tag:** Economics

**Q3 OTA earnings grid:**
| Company | Room Nights | Revenue/Bookings |
|---------|-------------|------------------|
| Booking Holdings | +7% | +10% |
| Expedia | +7% | +12% |
| Airbnb | ADR +4-6% | Mobile +29% |
| American Express | T&E +10% | Airlines +13% |

**Quote callout:** "Revenue growth is increasingly carried by higher prices and a smaller group of affluent travelers." - Skift

**Supporting stat:** Skift Travel Health Index = 100 (flat YoY volume)

**Visual direction:** Side-by-side comparison showing volume flat while revenue climbs

**Source:** Booking Holdings, Expedia, McKinsey, Skift

## Slide 5: Trend 3. AI Planning Hits Inflection Point

**Hero stat:** 56% (U.S. travelers using AI, up from 43%)
**Tag:** AI

**The Trust Gap visual:**
- 91% use AI to plan
- 2% trust AI to book

**Usage breakdown (potential pie or bar):**
- Recommendations: 75%
- Itinerary planning: 70%
- Discovery: 69%
- Booking: 13%

**Generational splits (potential horizontal bars):**
- Under 45: 65%
- Gen X: 44%
- Boomers: 29%

**Quote callout:** "The fastest behavioral shift the travel industry has seen in more than a decade." - PhocusWright

**Visual direction:** Large contrast between 91% (planning) and 2% (booking trust)

**Source:** PhocusWright, Klook Travel Pulse 2026, Deloitte

---

## Slide 6: Trend 4. OTAs Prove AI Users Convert at 2x Rate

**Hero stat:** 2x (conversion rate of AI users vs non-users)
**Secondary stat:** +20% (higher booking values for AI users)
**Tag:** AI

**Key points:**
- Booking Holdings Q3 2026 earnings: AI assistant users convert at double the rate
- Agoda AI users have booking values ~20% higher
- Google working with Booking.com, Expedia, Marriott, IHG to bring bookings into AI Mode

**DMO response stats:**
- 64% of DMOs write content for AI engines
- 51% concerned about or responding to AI impact

**Insight callout:** "The conversion data is in. AI users are better customers."

**Visual direction:** Large "2x" with supporting context; could show conversion funnel

**Source:** Booking Holdings Q3 2026, Sojern State of Destination Marketing

## Slide 8: Trend 6. The B2B Pivot Is Real

**Hero stat:** +19% (Expedia B2B growth)
**Comparison stat:** +7% (Expedia B2C growth)
**Tag:** Business

**Key points:**
- B2B revenue growth of 23% at Expedia
- Strategy: Becoming "one-stop travel shop" for partners
- OTA bookings generated $408 billion in 2026

**Industry context:**
- Tours & activities only 33% online booked vs 64% for travel overall
- Experiences market: $253B (2024) to $342B (2029)

**Insight callout:** "The next growth wave isn't consumer acquisition. It's powering other businesses."

**Visual direction:** B2B vs B2C comparison bars; runway opportunity chart

**Source:** Expedia Q3 2026, PhocusWright

## Slide 9: Data Points That Matter

Deep-cut statistics grid. Four categories:

**Consumer Behavior:**
- 81% choose destination by feeling, not location
- Pet-friendly bookings +260% YoY
- Marathon tourism: +300% Chicago, +228% Boston

**Loyalty Shifts:**
- 41% prefer hotel loyalty over travel credit cards
- Hilton reduced Diamond from 60 to 50 nights

**Search Behavior:**
- Google travel searches down ~10% (AI capturing discovery)
- Flight transaction value: +25% Canada, +20% Italy

**Cruise Industry:**
- 39.1M passengers forecast 2026
- 31% of recent cruisers were new-to-cruise (+12 pts)

**Visual direction:** Four quadrant grid or card layout with icons

**Source:** Tripadvisor, Forbes Advisor, CLIA, Skift

## Slide 10: Recommended Actions

Six numbered action cards:

1. **Optimize for the affluent traveler** - Revenue 2:1 over volume; premium positioning essential
2. **Make your content AI-legible** - 64% of DMOs already doing this; test in ChatGPT/Perplexity
3. **Build the AI-to-booking handoff** - 91% plan, 2% book; bridge the gap
4. **Re-evaluate your channel mix** - Display 75% to 45%; YouTube up to 55%; TikTok down to 28%
5. **Prepare for 2027 acceleration** - +8% international growth forecast
6. **Consider B2B partnerships** - Expedia B2B growing 3x faster than B2C

**Visual direction:** Numbered cards with bold headlines and one-line supporting context

## Slide 11: If You Do Nothing Else

Three centered priority actions:

1. **Run the AI audit** - Query ChatGPT and Perplexity with "[your destination] vacation planning"
2. **Know your affluent traveler** - Pull your top 20% of guests by spend
3. **Watch the 2027 forecasts** - Build campaign capacity for the rebound

**Visual direction:** Three large numbered items, minimal text, action-oriented

## Design Notes

- **Data-heavy month:** More charts and statistics than previous months
- **Two visual themes:** Cost/inflation (red/amber tones) and AI (blue/teal tones)
- **Contrast points:** 91% vs 2% (trust gap), B2B vs B2C growth, 2026 vs 2027 outlook
- **Quote callouts:** Use for PhocusWright and Skift attribution
- **Hero stats:** Each trend slide should have one dominant number
