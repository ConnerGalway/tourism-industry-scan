## Slide 3: Trend 1 — The Domestic Tourism Boom

**Core stat:** 70% of Canadians avoiding US travel

**Key data points:**
- 92% plan at least one domestic trip in 2026
- $4.4 billion in reshored spending projected through 2027
- Political tensions and tariff fears driving domestic preference
- "Canada first" sentiment at unprecedented levels

**Insight:** "This isn't a temporary blip—it's a structural shift in Canadian travel behavior. Destinations that capture these travelers now build loyalty that lasts."

**Sources:** BDC Canadian Travel Survey 2026, Destination Canada

## Slide 4: Trend 2 — Record Q3 Performance

**Core stat:** 3.1 million visitors in July 2026

**Key data points:**
- $140.9 billion in projected visitor spending for 2026
- 6% increase over 2025, beating the 5.4% forecast
- August overseas arrivals surged 7.2% year-over-year
- On track for $216.3 billion by 2035

**Insight:** "Q3 2026 proved the recovery thesis. International demand is back, domestic is surging, and the momentum is carrying into Q4."

**Sources:** Statistics Canada, Destination Canada, CEIC Data

## Slide 5: Trend 3 — Canadians Go Global Again

**Core stat:** Only 61% plan domestic travel this fall (down from 77% in summer)

**Key data points:**
- Mexico bookings jumped from 4% to 9% of fall travelers
- International destinations rose from 15% to 23%
- Casablanca bookings surged +250% YoY
- Delhi overtook Tokyo as #1 international city destination
- Costa Rica +145%, Aruba +118%

**Insight:** "The summer's domestic surge is softening. Canadians are rediscovering the world—and your destination is now competing with Morocco, not just Florida."

**Sources:** Léger Fall 2026, FlightHub, Statistics Canada

---

## Slide 6: Trend 4 — The Trade War Tourism Reversal

**Core stat:** 60% of Canadians less open to US travel than at any point in the past year

**Key data points:**
- Canadian visits to the US fell 25% in 2025 vs 2023-24
- That drop cost American border communities 14,000-42,000 jobs
- USD at ~C$1.37 makes Canada a high-value destination for Americans
- US-to-Canada travel surging as Americans seek value
- September cross-border trips hit 1.9 million (+6.8% YoY)

**Insight:** "The trade war has triggered a rare tourism reversal. While Canadians boycott the US, Americans are flooding north. Your next big market might be across the border."

**Sources:** ProMarket, CTV News, Léger, Statistics Canada

---

## Slide 7: Trend 5 — Hotel Rate-Driven Growth

**Core stat:** 8 consecutive months of RevPAR growth

**Key data points:**
- National occupancy: 80.9%
- ADR: CAD 267.48 (+6.7%)
- RevPAR: CAD 216.31 (+6.9%)
- Toronto led with ADR +10% to CAD 308
- Quebec RevPAR +10.1%, Ontario +8.2%

**Insight:** "Growth is coming from rate increases, not occupancy. The market supports premium pricing—don't lead with discounts."

**Sources:** STR/CoStar Canada Hotel Performance August 2026

## Slide 8: Trend 6 — 2027 Travel Predictions

**Core stat:** 69% want "out of character" vacations in 2027

**Key data points:**
- Travelers seeking trips that would "surprise friends and family"
- 39% of couples would travel specifically to resolve conflict
- 88% would take long-haul journeys if they'd arrive rested
- Transformation over relaxation is the 2027 theme

**Insight:** "Position your destination as a place for transformation, not just escape. Show people doing things they'd never do at home."

**Sources:** Booking.com Travel Predictions 2027

## Slide 9: Recommended Actions

Six numbered prescriptive steps:

1. **Capitalize on domestic boom** — Target Canadians who previously vacationed in the US; create "Why fly south?" campaigns
2. **Prepare for outbound competition** — The "stay home" momentum is fading; you're now competing with Morocco, not just Florida
3. **Capture American visitors** — Target US border states with "your dollar goes further" messaging; exchange rate does the selling
4. **Leverage premium pricing** — Partner on value-add packages rather than discounts; the market supports premium rates
5. **Target transformation seekers** — Feature experiences that challenge visitors; "out of character" messaging resonates
6. **Monitor TikTok ROI** — If you can't prove performance, reallocate to measured channels
